Social Justice, Technology, and Media

SJTM is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access academic journal dedicated to publishing high-quality scholarly research, critical discussions, and innovative contributions across relevant academic disciplines. The journal provides an inclusive platform for researchers, academics, scholars, and practitioners to share original research, exchange knowledge, and contribute to the advancement of academic and professional practice. SCS is committed to academic excellence, editorial independence, research integrity, transparency, and the unrestricted dissemination of scholarly knowledge. All scholarly articles published by SCS are freely accessible online immediately upon publication, without subscription fees, registration requirements, or embargo periods.

Aims and Scope

The primary aim of SJTM is to advance scholarly knowledge by providing a credible international platform for the dissemination of original social justice research. The journal seeks to encourage theoretical, empirical, and interdisciplinary scholarship that is inclusive to the Global South, and promote emerging community-based researchers to amplify their impact.
SJTM welcomes submissions addressing social justice in relation to new digital technologies such as social media, artificial intelligence, online learning, in addition to diverse media and the arts-based impacts on social justice in communities.

Author Support, Fees, and Publication Schedule

SJTM charges 0 fees to authors and readers regardless of country of origin based on our existing funding. Additionally, SJTM offers author support to students and community organizations who want to publish their first research article but require additional guidance. Publications and author support are provide on a monthly basis; please see the end of this page for more information.

Open Access Policy

Open Access Policy SJTM is a fully open-access journal. The journal follows the principles of open access by providing immediate, free, and unrestricted access to all published scholarly articles. Copyright holders grant users the right to access, read, download, copy, distribute, print, search, link to, and otherwise use published scholarly works for any lawful purpose, subject to the terms of the applicable open license. Specifically: 1) All scholarly articles are freely available immediately upon publication; 2) No subscription or payment is required to access articles; 3) No registration or user account is required to read or download articles; 4) No embargo period applies; 5) Users may share and distribute published articles in accordance with their respective licenses; 6) Published content is available in full text in PDF format.

Length: Minimum of 2,500 words, excluding references and abstract.

Authors should ensure that the language and references are error-free.

Peer Review and Editorial Process

SJTM applies a rigorous and transparent peer-review process to maintain the academic quality, originality, and integrity of published research.

Type of Peer Review

The journal uses a double-anonymous peer-review process. Authors and reviewers remain anonymous to one another throughout the review process.

Review Procedure

  1. Initial Editorial Assessment: Submitted manuscripts are examined by the editorial office to determine whether they meet the journal's scope, submission requirements, originality standards, and basic academic quality criteria.
  2. Plagiarism and Research Integrity Screening: Manuscripts may be screened using appropriate plagiarism-detection software and assessed for potential ethical concerns.
  3. Reviewer Assignment: Manuscripts passing the initial assessment are assigned to at least two independent reviewers with appropriate subject expertise.
  4. Independent Peer Review: Reviewers assess the manuscript's originality, research methodology, academic contribution, quality of analysis, presentation, and relevance.
  5. Editorial Decision: Based on the reviewers' reports, the editor may decide to accept, request minor revisions, request major revisions, or reject the manuscript.
  6. Revision and Reassessment: Revised manuscripts may be returned to the original reviewers for further assessment.
  7. Final Decision: The Editor-in-Chief makes the final publication decision, taking the reviewers' recommendations into consideration.
  8. Copyediting and Publication: Accepted manuscripts undergo editorial preparation and are published online as open-access articles.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are based exclusively on scholarly merit, originality, relevance, research quality, and compliance with publication ethics.

Authors' institutional affiliations, nationality, gender, personal relationships, or financial circumstances do not determine editorial decisions.

Special Issues

Special issues are subject to the same quality standards and peer-review requirements as regular journal issues.

The Editor-in-Chief retains ultimate responsibility for all published content, including special issues.

Guest editors must have appropriate academic qualifications and subject expertise and must be approved by the Editor-in-Chief.

Special issue manuscripts undergo independent peer review by at least two qualified reviewers. Guest editors' own submissions are handled independently, without their involvement in editorial decisions.

Special issue content is clearly identified on the journal website.

Instructions for Authors

SJTM welcomes original and unpublished scholarly manuscripts that fall within the journal's aims and scope.

Manuscript Categories

The journal considers:

  • Original research articles.
  • Systematic and literature reviews.
  • Theoretical and conceptual papers.
  • Methodological research.
  • Scholarly review essays, where relevant to the journal's scope.

General Submission Requirements

Authors must ensure that:

  • The manuscript is original and has not been published previously.
  • The manuscript is not simultaneously under consideration by another journal.
  • All authors have approved the submitted version.
  • All sources are appropriately cited and referenced.
  • Research methods are described clearly and accurately.
  • Ethical approval has been obtained where required.
  • Conflicts of interest are disclosed.
  • Funding sources are acknowledged.
  • Permissions have been obtained for third-party copyrighted material where necessary.

Manuscript Preparation

Manuscripts should include:

  1. Title.
  2. Author names and institutional affiliations.
  3. Corresponding author's contact details.
  4. Abstract.
  5. Keywords.
  6. Main text.
  7. References.
  8. Acknowledgements, funding, and declarations where applicable.

Additional formatting and referencing requirements are available in the journal's manuscript template.

Copyright Policy

Copyright ownership remains with the author unless a separate agreement explicitly states otherwise.

The copyright terms for scholarly articles are separate from the copyright terms applicable to the journal website and its design, branding, and other website materials.

No published open-access scholarly article is subject to an all-rights-reserved restriction.

Author Fees and Charges

SJTM is committed to transparent publishing costs.

SJTM does not charge authors any fees at any stage of the publication process.

There are no:

  • Submission fees.
  • Editorial processing charges.
  • Article processing charges (APCs).
  • Page charges.
  • Colour figure charges.
  • Publication or production fees.

All articles are published and made freely available to readers without cost to authors.

Conflicts of Interest

Authors, reviewers, and editors must disclose any financial, professional, institutional, or personal relationships that could influence their work or editorial judgment.

Editors with conflicts of interest must withdraw from handling the relevant manuscript.
